Hey everyone,

It has been a year since [my last update](/news/2025-honest-update), and I wanted to briefly check in. I can't believe how quickly it has gone by. Time away from working on the game has genuinely been good for me, and I've especially done a lot of things to address other aspects of my life that indirectly affect development, such as my health.

The project is still very much not cancelled, and I'm pleased to report that it is coming out of haitus as I've gotten back into a more regular gamedev schedule. Though I don't have anything exciting to share just yet, I've been mostly re-establishing my workflow and getting back into the mindset of creating.

Unfortunately, **Early Access will be delayed (again) until 2027**. I'd like to keep a date floating above my head, even if it is not set in stone.

Thank you as always for your patience, understanding, and kind feedback on the demo. I hope to have another update--a proper progress update--soon!

*\- Jaiden*
